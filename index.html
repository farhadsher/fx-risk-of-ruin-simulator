What do these numbers mean?
Risk of ruin
% of simulated runs where balance ever fell to (or below) your ruin threshold.
Value at Risk (5%)
The final balance below which the worst 5% of runs fall — a "reasonable worst case".
Conditional VaR (5%)
The average final balance across only that worst 5% of runs — how bad the bad cases average out to.
Ulcer Index
A blended measure of both the depth and duration of drawdowns (not just the single worst dip). Lower is smoother.
Kelly criterion
The risk % that theoretically maximizes long-run compound growth, given your edge. Betting more than 1× Kelly increases risk of ruin faster than it increases returns; most traders target a fraction of Kelly (e.g. 0.25–0.5×) for a smoother ride.
Bootstrap simulation
Instead of assuming a fixed win rate and reward:risk, this mode randomly resamples (with replacement) from your own past R-multiples — capturing the real shape of your results, including partial wins/losses.
Streakiness / correlation
Real trading results are sometimes not independent — a losing streak can mean a regime change, not bad luck. This lets you stress-test that.

This tool runs a Monte Carlo simulation of your trading strategy under simplifying assumptions (fixed-fractional position sizing on current balance; in theoretical mode, trades are independent and identically distributed unless you add correlation; costs are approximated as a flat percent of risk per trade). Real markets, real execution, and real psychology can differ — this shows the shape of the risk, not a guarantee of future results. This is for educational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Everything runs locally in your browser — no data is sent anywhere, including anything you paste into the trade history box.
